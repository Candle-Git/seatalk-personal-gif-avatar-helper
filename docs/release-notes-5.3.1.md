# SeaTalk 表情助手 5.3.1 · 从聊天收藏

拖选表情时，消息保持可见。输入框工具栏会显示已选数量，点「预览」可核对表情、选分组或加关键字，点 × 可退出。

## SeaTalk Sticker Helper 5.3.1 · Collect from chat

Messages stay visible as you drag to select stickers. The chat toolbar shows your selection count. Choose **Preview** to review stickers, pick a group or add keywords. Choose **×** to exit.

[Install or update](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/588931)
