# SeaTalk 表情助手 5.3.0 · 在聊天中快选，在分组里整理

打开一个单聊或群聊，点击输入框工具栏里的折角笑脸图标。**表情快选**会显示当前接收人，你可以搜索全部收藏、连续滚动，点一张直接发送。要一次发送多张，进入**管理表情**，选好后核对接收人和图片，再确认发送。

- **从聊天收藏**。拖过多条消息，预览要收藏的表情，选好分组或输入搜索关键字后一起保存。纯文字会跳过，同一张表情只计一次。
- **批量整理**。按 Ctrl／⌘ 逐张增减，按 Shift 连选，或在空白处拖动框选。选中的表情可以统一改名、移组或追加关键字。覆盖旧名称前会显示影响张数。
- **按组查找**。初次打开管理页时显示「未分组」。其他分组可以横向滚动，「全部」固定在最左侧，点它即可跨组搜索。

**从 5.2 升级**。如果还有旧个人备份需要恢复，请先在 5.2 中导入，再原地更新。5.3 使用 `STGIF3` 分享口令和第 3 版个人备份。升级后请重新生成分享口令，换机前重新导出备份。旧的表情文字和私人说明仍可在原浏览器搜索。需要独立副本时请保留 5.2 的原备份。

## SeaTalk Sticker Helper 5.3.0 · Quick stickers and groups

Open a direct or group chat and click the folded-corner smiley beside the chat input. **Quick stickers** shows the recipient and searches all your favorites. Scroll through the results and click a sticker to send it immediately. For a batch, open **Manage stickers**, select the stickers, then check the recipient and images before confirming.

- **Collect from chat.** Drag across messages, review the stickers and save them together with an optional group or search keywords. Text messages are skipped and duplicate stickers count once.
- **Organize a selection.** Ctrl-click or ⌘-click to add or remove individual stickers, Shift-click to select a range, or drag from empty space to select an area. Rename the batch, move it to a group or append keywords. The helper shows how many custom names a rename would replace.
- **Search across groups.** The manager opens to Ungrouped. Scroll sideways through your groups. All stays pinned on the left so you can search the entire collection.

**Upgrading from 5.2.** Restore any old personal backup in 5.2 before updating in place. Version 5.3 uses `STGIF3` share codes and version 3 personal backups. Generate fresh codes after upgrading and export a new backup before moving to another computer. Previously saved sticker text and private notes remain searchable in the original browser. Keep your original 5.2 backup if you need a separate copy of those fields.
